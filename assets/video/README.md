# 30-second program walkthrough — production kit

## How the video is made
1. **Voiceover** — record or generate the script below as one audio file (MP3 or WAV).
2. **Footage** — generate the 6 clips below on Higgsfield. The clips are background footage only, with no words in them.
3. **Motion graphics** — Claude adds every piece of information on top of the clips as animated graphics. This covers the titles, step numbers, portal-style cards, price and on-screen captions, all timed to the voiceover. AI video tools scramble on-screen words, so the text is kept out of the generated footage.
4. The finished MP4 plays when visitors click **"Watch the program walkthrough"** (home page) and **"Open the welcome walkthrough"** (welcome guide).

## Voiceover script (about 28 seconds)
Voice: warm, calm and reassuring, at a relaxed pace of about 145 words per minute, with a short pause between scenes. A Canadian English accent is ideal.

| Time | Scene | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Intro | Rebuilding your credit doesn't have to feel overwhelming. Here's how CreditPulse works. |
| 0:05–0:10 | Step 1 | First, upload your Equifax and TransUnion reports to your secure portal. |
| 0:10–0:15 | Step 2 | AI flags anything that may need a closer look, and you confirm what's accurate. |
| 0:15–0:20 | Step 3 | We prepare dispute letters for you to review. Nothing is sent without your approval. |
| 0:20–0:25 | Step 4 | Then follow every response and your next step, all in one place. |
| 0:25–0:30 | End | CreditPulse. You review. You approve. You stay in control. |

## Higgsfield clips
Settings for all clips:
- **Mode:** image-to-video.
- **Format:** 16:9, 1080p if available.
- **Length:** 5 seconds.
- **Motion:** low or subtle camera motion.

If there is a negative prompt box, paste: `text, words, letters, logos, watermark, extra people, distorted hands, distorted face, warping, flicker`
