What does CreditPulse help me do?

CreditPulse helps organise your credit reports, flag potential issues for your review, prepare letters for approval and track case responses. Your input is required to confirm details and decide what to dispute.

Does AI make the decisions for me?

No. A flagged finding is something to review, not proof that an error exists. You confirm the facts, answer questions and review each letter before approving it.

What should I have ready?

Your Equifax and TransUnion consumer reports, plus the identification and address documents requested in the authenticated client portal. The welcome guide explains the preparation steps. Please don’t send these documents through this introduction site.

Is a higher credit score guaranteed?

No. Results depend on your individual credit history, whether information is inaccurate, bureau responses and your ongoing financial activity. No specific score increase, deletion or completion date is promised.

How often is the membership billed?

The proposed membership is $19.97 CAD every 14 days. That means every two weeks, rather than twice a month. Your amount due, any applicable tax, renewal date and cancellation terms need to be shown before a live payment is confirmed. This preview does not charge you.

What happens after I join?

You begin with the welcome guide, review the required permissions and prepare your reports. The authenticated portal is where you submit documents, review findings and manage your case. You can preview the welcome guide here without joining.
